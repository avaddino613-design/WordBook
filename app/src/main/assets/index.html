    Home screen widget

    Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets, and pick Wordbook. The widget follows the card options above, except "Hide the meaning until I tap". Tap the widget to open the app, or tap its shuffle icon for another word. The word changes over to a new one each day.

    Home screen shortcut

    Open this link in your phone's browser and choose Add to Home screen. It opens straight to the word, with no menus. A web page can't be a live widget on the home screen itself.

    Your data

    Words are saved in this app, on this device. Copy a backup and keep it in a note so you can restore your words if the app is ever reinstalled.
